Our Services

End-to-end support, from sourcing to your port of destination.

Sourcing & Procurement

We work directly with growers and trusted processors across India's major spice, seed and pulses growing regions to secure consistent, quality raw material.

Quality Grading & Testing

Every batch is checked for purity, moisture, admixture and grain size before it is cleared for packing, so buyers receive exactly what was quoted.

Custom Packaging

PP bags, jumbo bags or buyer-branded packaging in the sizes and formats required for retail or bulk industrial supply.

Export Documentation

We handle the paperwork — invoices, certificates of origin, phytosanitary and other export documentation required by your country.

Logistics & Shipping

Coordinated container loading and shipping schedules to help your consignment reach port on time, every time.

Dedicated Buyer Support

A single point of contact for quotes, order tracking and after-sales queries via call, email or WhatsApp.
